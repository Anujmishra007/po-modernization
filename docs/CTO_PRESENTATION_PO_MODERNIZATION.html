The Challenge

Why We Need to Modernize

Our legacy PO module has accumulated 20 years of technical debt, making it increasingly difficult to maintain, test, and extend.

⚠️ Monolithic SPs

Single stored procedures containing 1,800-3,000 lines mixing validation, business logic, and persistence. Impossible to unit test or modify safely.

⚠️ NOLOCK Everywhere

Dirty reads throughout the codebase leading to data inconsistencies. SELECT statements inside transactions causing phantom reads.

⚠️ No Compensation

When step 5 of 10 fails, steps 1-4 remain committed. Manual cleanup required, often resulting in orphaned data.

⚠️ Client IF-ELSE Chains

200+ lines of IF-ELSE per SP to handle 50+ client variations. Adding a new client requires modifying 20+ stored procedures.

⚠️ Dynamic SQL

String concatenation building SQL at runtime: EXEC(@sql + @param). Security risk and plan cache bloat.

⚠️ Zero Observability

No tracing, no metrics, no logging standards. Debugging requires direct database access and guesswork.
